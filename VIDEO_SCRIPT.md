# Renewal Clock demo script

The captioned video (docs/demo-captioned.mp4, 2:32, no sound) shows these lines in order. Read them as a voice-over if you record one.

1. Your lease ends January 31. When is the last day to tell your landlord? It is December 2, two months earlier.
2. Every contract has a day like that: a phone promo that ends, a gym that renews itself, an alarm contract with 60 days' notice.
3. Miss it and you pay. Renewal Clock finds that day for each one, using Ontario and Canadian rules.
4. Leases and contracts are private. Everything stays on your device, and a security policy stops the page from sending anything anywhere.
5. Here is a made up Toronto household with five contracts.
6. The next deadline sits on top. The gym membership signed five days ago can still be cancelled, for any reason, until November 3.
7. Each contract gets a countdown to its last day to act, sorted soonest first. Red is this week.
8. It also adds up what is at stake: $1,920 a year in price jumps and renewals.
9. The lease. Ontario tenants give 60 days' notice on Form N9, ending on the last day of a rental period.
10. So January 31 means notice by December 2. February and March have their own exceptions, and it knows them.
11. It says what happens if you miss it, in plain words, then the steps, and a cover note ready to send.
12. The phone plan. The promo ends November 20 and the bill goes from $55 to $80. That is $300 a year.
13. Under the CRTC Wireless Code, the cancellation fee falls every month. Leave today and the most they can charge is $140.
14. The dates on the way, with a nudge to call about the promo three weeks before it ends.
15. The whole year on one line. Red dots are deadlines.
16. Adding a contract takes one paste. Here is a gym agreement.
17. It reads the wording on your device and highlights every fact it used: the dates, auto renewal, 30 days' notice, the $600 paid up front.
18. Nothing is hidden. Check each one, fix anything in the form, and save.
19. Ontario law protects gym members: 10 days to cancel, a one year limit, and a renewal notice 30 to 90 days before the end.
20. Add to calendar gives you a file with every deadline and reminders a week and a day before. It works with Google, Apple and Outlook.
21. Every rule links its source: the Landlord and Tenant Board, the CRTC codes and Ontario's consumer rules. And it says what it cannot do.
22. Plain JavaScript, no server, no account, free on GitHub Pages. 16 tests and a browser check.
23. Renewal Clock. Know the last day, before it passes. Made by John Khan in Toronto, Ontario.
